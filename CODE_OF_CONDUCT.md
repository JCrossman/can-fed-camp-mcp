# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project welcoming and harassment-free,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status,
nationality, appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behaviour

Be respectful, assume good intent while addressing impact, accept constructive
feedback, and prioritize accessibility and citizen safety. Harassment, threats,
sexualized attention, discriminatory language, doxxing, and publishing another
person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the repository
owner's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
ban participants whose conduct is inappropriate. This code applies in project
spaces and when someone officially represents the project.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
